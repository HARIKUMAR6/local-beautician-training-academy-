About Sorrel

A working studio that teaches the craft.

We train makeup, skin, hair and nail artists the way the trade works: at the chair, on real faces.

Explore courses Visit the studio

02 · Who we are

We don’t teach looks. We teach the hands that make them.

Sorrel is a beauty school built like a studio. Trends change; technique lasts.

  1. iTechnique firstSkin prep, colour and structure before style.
  2. iiHands on, every weekPractice fills most of the timetable.
  3. iiiHonest feedbackClear notes on every piece of work.

03 · How we teach

One method, four steps, every lesson.

The same rhythm runs through every discipline, so progress is easy to see.

  1. Step 01

    Watch

    A trainer demonstrates live, step by step.

  2. Step 02

    Practise

    You repeat it on a model at your own station.

  3. Step 03

    Check

    Your trainer reviews the detail with you.

  4. Step 04

    Finish

    The finished look goes into your portfolio.

04 · The studio

Four rooms, one studio floor.

Each discipline has its own room, set up like a professional workplace.
