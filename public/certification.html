Certification

Certified by what you can do.

Complete your course, log your practice and pass a practical assessment to receive your Sorrel certificate of completion.

See the requirements Browse courses

02 · Requirements

What you need to complete.

Four things, checked by your trainers as you go.

  1. 01AttendAt least 90% of scheduled sessions.
  2. 02Log your practiceEvery required practical hour, signed off by a trainer.
  3. 03Build a portfolioPhotographed finished work covering the course looks.
  4. 04Pass the practicalA final assessment on a live model.

03 · Assessment

How the practical is assessed.

One session, on a live model, observed by Sorrel trainers.

  1. 1

    Prepare

    Plan the look and set up a clean, safe station.

  2. 2

    Perform

    Carry out the work within the set time.

  3. 3

    Review

    Talk the assessor through your choices and the finish.

04 · Criteria

What the assessor looks for.

You pass at Competent or above in every criterion.

Not there yet? You can re-sit the practical after more supervised practice.
