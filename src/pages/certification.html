02 · Requirements

What you need to complete.

Four things, checked by your trainers as you go.

  1. {{icon:check}}01AttendAt least 90% of scheduled sessions.
  2. {{icon:check}}02Log your practiceEvery required practical hour, signed off by a trainer.
  3. {{icon:check}}03Build a portfolioPhotographed finished work covering the course looks.
  4. {{icon:check}}04Pass the practicalA final assessment on a live model.
